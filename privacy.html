NEON VOID

Privacy Policy  •  Last updated: June 27, 2026

Neon Void is a single-player arcade game with an optional online save feature. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what is stored and where.

No ads · No third-party trackers · No data sold

What stays on your device

By default, everything is stored locally on your device using your browser/app storage. This includes your game progress, coins, gems, unlocked cosmetics, high scores, achievements, and settings. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable cloud save.

Optional cloud save (account)

If you choose to create an account inside the game, the following is sent to and stored on our save server (hosted on Cloudflare Workers KV) so your progress can sync across devices:

  • A username you pick. This is a self-chosen display handle, not your real name. You are not required to provide your real identity, email, or phone number.
  • Your game progress (coins, gems, unlocks, scores, and similar gameplay state).

If you set a password, it is never stored or transmitted in plain text — only a one-way cryptographic hash is used to authenticate writes to your save. We do not use this information to identify you as a person, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No precise or coarse location.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No browsing history or cross-app/website tracking.

How the data is used

Cloud-save data is used for one purpose only: to store and restore your game progress when you sign in. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Network requests the game makes are limited to our own save server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Children's privacy

Neon Void does not knowingly collect personal information from children, contains no behavioral advertising, and does no cross-app tracking. The only optional data is a self-chosen username and gameplay progress.

Deleting your data

  • Local data: delete the app, or clear the app's/site's storage, to remove all on-device progress.
  • Cloud save: to have a cloud save associated with your username deleted, contact us at the address below and include the username.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
